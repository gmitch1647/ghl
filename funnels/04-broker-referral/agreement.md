## 1. What this is

You are an independent marketing affiliate. You refer business owners to Dream Gate
Financial for business funding products. You are not an employee, contractor for services,
loan broker of record, or agent of the Company. You have no authority to bind the Company,
quote rates, negotiate terms, or make promises about underwriting outcomes.

## 2. What you get paid

- **Rate:** 2.0% of the gross funded amount on each Qualifying Referral.
- **Qualifying Referral:** a business that (a) clicked your unique tracking link or was
  submitted via your unique referral form within the prior 90 days, (b) was NOT already a
  contact in Dream Gate Financial's CRM before that first click, (c) funds a business
  funding product through Dream Gate Financial, and (d) completes the funded transaction
  without cancellation, refund, or clawback within 30 days of funding.
- **Eligible products:** Merchant Cash Advance, business term loans, 0% business credit
  card stacking, and other business funding products explicitly listed in your affiliate
  portal. Credit repair services and personal credit products are NOT eligible.
- **Self-referrals:** You cannot earn commission on funding your own business or any
  business in which you have ownership.
- **Minimum payout:** $50. Balances below the minimum carry over to the next payout cycle.

## 3. When you get paid

- Commissions are calculated on the 1st of each month for funded deals from the prior month.
- Payment is issued net-30 from the funded date.
- Payout methods: ACH (preferred), PayPal, or paper check (US addresses only).
- **W-9 required before first payout.** No W-9 = no payout. If your annual commissions exceed
  $600, we issue a Form 1099-NEC by January 31 of the following year.

## 4. Attribution

- Each Affiliate gets a unique tracking link.
- Attribution window: 90 days from a referred user's first click on your link.
- Last-touch-within-window: if a user clicks links from multiple affiliates, the affiliate
  whose link they last clicked within the 90-day window earns the commission.
- If a user submits a funding application via a form not tied to your link, no attribution.
- The Company's records of tracking and pipeline status are the final word on attribution.

## 5. What you can't do

- No spam (email, SMS, or otherwise). You must comply with CAN-SPAM and TCPA.
- No paid search bidding on Dream Gate Financial or related brand terms.
- No claiming you ARE Dream Gate Financial, an employee, or a representative.
- No quoting rates, approval amounts, or making guarantees about funding outcomes.
- No targeting consumers for personal-credit products.
- No referring businesses to which you have not honestly described our service.
- No false testimonials, fake reviews, or fabricated case studies.
- No referrals from prohibited industries (cannabis, adult, firearms, gambling, MLM
  recruitment, anything illegal in the borrower's state).

## 6. Marketing materials

You may use the Company's name, logo, and approved swipe copy ONLY in the manner described
in the affiliate portal. You may not modify the logo, create fake co-branding, or imply a
partnership beyond "affiliate."

## 7. Confidentiality

You agree not to share internal swipe copy, scripts, pricing, or commission structure
publicly. Sharing with referred prospects is fine; posting "Dream Gate pays 2%" in a public
Facebook group is not.

## 8. Termination

Either party may terminate this agreement at any time, for any reason, by emailing
gmitch1647@gmail.com. On termination:

- You stop using all tracking links and marketing assets within 5 business days.
- Commissions already earned and not yet paid are still paid out on the next scheduled cycle,
  subject to the clawback window.

The Company may terminate immediately and forfeit unpaid commissions if you breach Section 5
(prohibited conduct) or commit fraud.

## 9. Clawbacks

If a referred deal is refunded, charged back, cancelled, or proven fraudulent within 30 days
of funding, the corresponding commission is reversed and deducted from your next payout. If
your balance is insufficient, you agree to repay the Company within 30 days.

## 10. Independent contractor

You are an independent contractor. You are responsible for your own taxes, insurance, and
expenses. Nothing here creates a partnership, joint venture, employment, or agency
relationship.

## 11. Indemnification

You will indemnify and hold harmless Dream Gate Financial, its officers, employees, and
contractors from any claim arising out of your marketing activities, your breach of this
agreement, or your violation of any law.

## 12. Disclaimers

The Company makes no guarantee about your earnings. There is no minimum or expected income.
Funding outcomes depend on the prospect's business and our underwriting standards.

## 13. Changes

Continued participation after 30 days = acceptance.
