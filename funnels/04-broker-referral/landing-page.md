## Section 7 — FAQ (collapsed accordion)

**How much can I really make?**
There's no cap. Top broker partners send 5–10 funded deals a month. At an average $100K funded, that's $10K–$20K/month.

**How do I know my lead is being tracked?**
You get a unique link. Anyone who clicks it gets cookied for 90 days. When they fund, the commission attaches to your account. You also get an email the moment they enter our pipeline.

**When do I get paid?**
Net-30 from the funded date. Minimum payout is $50. ACH is fastest, PayPal also works, checks for US addresses only.

**Do I need a license to refer?**
No. You're a marketing affiliate, not a broker of record. You're paid for marketing, not for negotiating loan terms.

**Can I refer my own business?**
You can apply for funding yourself, but you can't earn commission on your own deal.

**What if my referral already had an account with you?**
First-touch wins. If they were already a contact in our CRM before your link sent them, the commission doesn't attach. We're transparent about this — you'll see the rejection reason in the email.
