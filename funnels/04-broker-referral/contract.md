# AFFILIATE AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT (the "Agreement") provides the terms and conditions of the relationship between Dreamgate Solutions LLC, a limited liability company (the "Company," "we," "us," or "our") and `{{contact.full_name}}` ("Affiliate," "you" or "your") in order to participate in the Dreamgate Solutions Affiliate Program (the "Program") to refer prospective clients to the Company's funding services.

WHEREAS, the Company provides business funding solutions and operates the website and related platforms under the Dreamgate Solutions LLC brand (collectively, the "Site"); and

WHEREAS, the Company has created a Program that enables Affiliates to refer prospective funding clients to the Company in exchange for agreed consideration; and

WHEREAS, you desire to participate in the Program, as evidenced by your application, which the Company may approve or disapprove in its sole discretion.

THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises herein, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1.  DEFINITIONS

Capitalized terms used and not otherwise defined in this Agreement shall have the following meanings:

(a) "Referred Client" means any person or business entity that is introduced or referred by the Affiliate to the Company and that submits a funding application to the Company.

(b) "Funded Transaction" means a transaction in which (i) a Referred Client has been approved for and has received funding arranged or facilitated by the Company, and (ii) the Company has actually received, in cleared funds, the commission, fee, or other compensation owed to the Company by the lender, funder, or Referred Client in connection with that funding.

(c) "Amount Funded" means the total principal amount of funding actually disbursed to the Referred Client in connection with a Funded Transaction, expressed in U.S. Dollars.

(d) "Links" means any banner, button, tracking link, referral code, intake form, or other manner approved by the Company by which a Referred Client is identified as having been referred by the Affiliate.

(e) "Affiliate Channel" means any website, social media account, email list, advertising network, or other marketing channel operated by the Affiliate and used to refer prospective clients to the Company.

## 2.  AFFILIATE CHANNEL AND CONTENT

You have sole and exclusive responsibility and liability for the development, operation, maintenance, and all materials and content that appear on the Affiliate Channel. You shall operate and maintain the Affiliate Channel in accordance with all applicable laws, rules, and regulations, including without limitation all federal and state laws governing commercial advertising, lead generation, and the marketing of financial products.

In consideration of the Fees, you agree to use the Links and approved marketing materials provided by the Company in accordance with the terms and conditions of this Agreement.

We reserve the right to monitor the Affiliate Channel to determine whether you are in compliance with this Agreement.

The Company is not responsible to pay any Fees in the event you do not use the Links provided to you by the Company without modification, or you do not properly install or use those Links, with the result that a referral cannot be properly tracked back to the Affiliate Channel.

## 3.  COMPENSATION FOR YOUR REFERRED CLIENTS

Subject to your proper use of the Links and the other terms of this Agreement, you will be compensated based upon Funded Transactions. Your compensation (the "Fee") shall be two percent (2%) of the Amount Funded for each Funded Transaction attributable to your referral.

PAYMENT IS CONTINGENT ON RECEIPT OF FUNDS BY THE COMPANY. No Fee shall be earned, accrued, or payable to the Affiliate unless and until the Company has actually received, in cleared and collected funds, the commission, fee, or other compensation owed to the Company by the lender, funder, or Referred Client in connection with the underlying funding. If the Company is not paid for any reason — including but not limited to client default, lender clawback, rescission, cancellation, dispute, fraud, or non-collection — the Company shall have no obligation to pay any Fee to the Affiliate with respect to that transaction, and any Fee previously paid in respect of such transaction shall be refundable to the Company or offsettable against future Fees.

Provided the Company has received payment as described above, Fees shall be disbursed to the Affiliate on a weekly basis.

The Fee shall be offset or reduced with respect to any Funded Transaction for which:

(a) the Company issues a discount, credit, refund, or allowance;

(b) there is a chargeback, clawback, rescission, or reversal of payment previously credited to the Company; or

(c) the Referred Client fails to pay, defaults, or the funding is otherwise unwound.

The Company reserves the right to refuse any prospective client or transaction in its sole and absolute discretion. You shall have no claim to any Fee based on the Company's decision not to complete a transaction with any Referred Client.

All Fees are payable in U.S. Dollars. If you are required to provide tax or other governmental documentation (including, without limitation, a properly completed IRS Form W-9 or W-8), payment of Fees may be suspended pending completion of such documentation.

## 4.  FULFILLMENT AND CLIENT INFORMATION

All information about funding transactions and Referred Clients is the sole and exclusive property of the Company.

Additionally, you agree that the Company may collect, process, and use certain information about you in connection with the administration of the Program.

You agree to use the Links and approved marketing materials on the Affiliate Channel. Links and marketing materials may contain logos, trademarks, and service marks (collectively, "Marks"). The Company grants you a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable, and revocable license to display the Links and Marks on the Affiliate Channel, and nowhere else, subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement.

You may not use the Links or the Marks for any other purpose absent the express written consent of the Company. You may not change, add to, or delete from the Links or Marks. You may not use the Marks independently of the Links for any purpose without the Company's express written consent, and you may not use or present the Links in any manner that suggests the endorsement of any other goods, services, persons, or entities without the express written consent of the Company.

You agree not to (a) acquire or attempt to acquire, register or attempt to register, or in any way use any domain names, trademarks, service marks, keywords, handles, screen names, or other forms of identification incorporating the Marks; or (b) use the Marks in any way that suggests the source of the Affiliate Channel is the Company.

All intellectual property rights in and to the Marks, and any goodwill generated by your use of the Marks, shall inure solely to the benefit of the Company. Upon suspension or termination of this Agreement, the rights granted under this section shall automatically be suspended or terminated, as applicable.

## 9.  TERM AND TERMINATION

This Agreement shall commence on the date of signing by both Parties and may be terminated by either Party at any time upon written notice to the other. If the Company terminates this Agreement due to a breach by you, no further Fees shall be paid to you, and the Company may pursue such other relief, equitable and legal, as may be available. If you terminate without cause, Fees properly earned and payable under Section 3 (and actually received by the Company) shall be paid as provided herein. Regardless of which Party terminates, upon termination, all rights and licenses granted by the Company to you shall immediately cease, and you shall immediately stop using and remove the Links and Marks from the Affiliate Channel.

You promise and agree to hold all Confidential Information of the Company in strict confidence and in trust for the sole benefit of the Company, both during the term of this Agreement and at all times thereafter, and shall not use such Confidential Information for any purpose other than as may be reasonably necessary for the performance of your duties under this Agreement, without the Company's prior written consent.

You shall not disclose any Confidential Information to any person or entity other than to such of your employees or contractors as may be reasonably necessary for purposes of performing your duties hereunder, and only where they have executed agreements of confidentiality no less protective than this Agreement. You shall use at least the same degree of care to protect Confidential Information as you use to protect your own confidential information, but in no event less than a reasonable degree of care.

Confidential Information does not include information that (a) is or becomes publicly known through lawful means; (b) was rightfully in your possession prior to the effective date of this Agreement; or (c) is disclosed to you without restriction by a third party who rightfully possesses the information.

If you are required to disclose Confidential Information by virtue of a lawful court order, subpoena, or similar legal request, you will promptly notify the Company in writing and cooperate so that the Company may seek an appropriate protective order.
