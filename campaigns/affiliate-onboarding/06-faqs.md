{{contact.first_name}},

Seven questions I get every week from new affiliates. Answers below.

**1. When do I actually get the money?**
Weekly. Every Friday we run payouts. Your commission joins that week's batch as soon as
we've collected our fee from the lender on your referred deal.

**2. Why isn't it the same day the deal funds?**
{{custom_values.affiliate_payment_contingency}} The lender owes us a commission on every
deal, and they don't pay it the same day they fund. Sometimes it's days, sometimes a
couple of weeks. The minute that money lands in our account, your portion is queued.

**3. What if the lender claws back or the deal defaults?**
The contract is explicit: no Fee is owed for a deal we don't actually collect on. If a
clawback happens after we've already paid you, that amount gets offset against future
payouts (or refunded if you have no future earnings). It's rare, but it's how the
underlying business works.

**4. What if my referral is already in your CRM?**
First-touch wins. If they were a contact in our system before clicking your link, no
commission attaches. You'll see this in your statement with the exact reason. We don't
hide it.

**5. Can I get a list of past clients to follow up with?**
No. You only earn on NEW business owners you bring into our system. Past clients are not
fair game.

**6. What if they fund 6 months later?**
The attribution window is {{custom_values.affiliate_attribution_window}} from their first
click. If they click today and fund 89 days from now → you get paid. Day 91 → you don't.

**7. Can I run paid ads to my affiliate link?**
Yes, but: no bidding on Dreamgate brand terms. And the FTC requires disclosure — your ads
need to make clear you're an affiliate.
