{{contact.first_name}},

Six questions I get every week from new affiliates. Answers below.

**1. What if my referral is already in your CRM?**
First-touch wins. If they were a contact in our system before clicking your link, no
commission attaches. You'll see this in your monthly statement with the exact reason. We
don't hide it.

**2. Can I get a list of past clients to follow up with?**
No. You only earn on NEW business owners you bring into our system. Past clients are not
fair game.

**3. What if they fund 6 months later?**
The attribution window is {{custom_values.affiliate_attribution_window}} from their first
click. If they click today and fund 89 days from now → you get paid. Day 91 → you don't.

**4. Can I run paid ads to my affiliate link?**
Yes, but: no bidding on Dream Gate Financial or related brand terms. And the FTC requires
disclosure — your ads need to make clear you're an affiliate.

**5. What if my referral fails underwriting?**
Then you don't get paid (we only pay on funded deals). But that contact is in our system —
if we re-engage them later and they fund within the attribution window, you're still
credited.

**6. How do I see my pending and paid commissions?**
For v1, we email you a monthly statement on the 1st of each month. A self-serve dashboard
is on the roadmap — when it ships, you'll be the first to know.
